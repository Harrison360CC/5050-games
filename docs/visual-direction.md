# 5050 Games: visual direction (Oct 22, 2026)

## Verdict
Receipt/ledger plus pixel: coherent and current. Receipt is the system, pixel is the voice: pixels only in the wordmark, sprites and tiny icons, never body copy or money. Budget goes into the ledger, which is the honesty mechanic. Hazard orange works as fill or rule; on light paper it fails as text, so use a darker ink-orange for light-mode accent text.

## Fonts (Google Fonts)
- Pixel display: Silkscreen (wide, keep wordmark short) or Jersey 10. Press Start 2P is arcade cliché.
- Money mono: Martian Mono (wide, totals on own line on phones) or IBM Plex Mono / Geist Mono.
- Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (institutional, suits a ledger) or Public Sans.
- Set font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on any proportional face used for numbers.

## Palette
Light: bg paper #EDEBE6, surface receipt #FAF9F6, ink #1A1A1A, muted #5F5D58, rule #C6C3BC, accent fill #F26B0A, accent text #B84A00.
Dark: bg concrete #161615, surface #222221, ink #ECEAE4, muted #A5A29B, rule #3B3A37, accent #FF8B2E (fill and text), on-accent ink #161615.
Contrast: light ink/bg 14.6:1, muted 5.5:1, accent fill on bg 2.6:1 (fill/large only), ink on accent 5.7:1, accent text on bg 4.4:1, on surface 5.0:1. Dark ink/bg 15.1:1, muted 7.1:1, accent on bg 7.8:1.
Why it reads hazard not crypto: warm grey not near-black, black text on orange (safety-sign convention), no second saturated colour, no glow, no green.

## Pot counter
Two receipt lines: "EST. POT" with dotted leader and a greyed "unconfirmed" stamp, then "CONFIRMED" in ink with date once ad revenue settles. Never tween digits; update on load with "as of Tue 14:02 AST". Show the split as line items: ad revenue, house cut, merch, seed, rollover, pot. Seed line reads "SEED (Harrison, out of pocket)". Under it: "last week paid: $X to @name". No coins, confetti, spinning numbers.

## Loyalty Center references
Severance (corridor rhythm, one off colour), Playtime 1967 (top-down cubicle grid), Brazil (exposed ducts, paper forms), Portal (numbered rooms, stick-figure warning signs), Nakagin Capsule Tower (pod silhouette), Playdead Inside (3-value flat palette, one red).
Art direction: 16px top-down tiles on concrete grey, 3-value ramp, lime-grey floor tape for queues, pods as 2x3 tile boxes with one lit accent-orange screen pixel, fluorescent tubes as one light line, vending machine and water cooler as the only props, pictogram signage in the pixel face, one employee sprite that walks slightly too slowly. No neon, no chrome, no purple light.

## Avoid
Cream + serif + terracotta. Near-black + lone acid green. Any gradient hero. Centred hero stacks (left-align like a receipt). Rounded cards with accent rail (square corners, hairline rules). Gambling tells: gold, green felt, chips, coins, odometer rolls, "WIN BIG", countdowns, red/green arrows, jackpot animation. No rendered or painted imagery; everything is type, rules, or hand-placed pixels.
