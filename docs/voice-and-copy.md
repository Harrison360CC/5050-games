# 5050 Games: brand voice and draft copy (Oct 22, 2026)

## Draft site copy

Homepage hero
- Headline: Play badly. Someone still wins.
- Line: Three one-tap games, one weekly leaderboard each, and the top score takes half of whatever the ads made.

Pot counter
- Label: THIS WEEK'S POT (so far)
- Under it: Half the ad money from this game, counted nightly. It is not a lot. It was not meant to be.

Game cards
- Drunk Pigeon: Tap to flap. The pigeon has had a few. So has the gravity.
- Rent's Due: Run. The landlord is faster than you think and the month is shorter.
- Slumlord: Stack units on units until the inspector shows up or physics does.

How it works
1. Play. Scores count on Ranked, not Practice.
2. Top score at Sunday 23:59 UTC wins half that game's ad money for the week, plus half the merch profit.
3. We pay out within 7 days, after the ad network confirms the numbers.
4. Nobody plays, nobody wins, the pot rolls into next week.

Leaderboard empty state: Nobody has scored yet. Technically, zero is winning.

Practice vs Ranked
- Practice: no leaderboard, no pot, no pressure.
- Ranked: one run counts. Every run counts. Sign in first.

Sign-in prompt: We need an email so we know who to pay. That is the whole reason. No newsletter unless you ask for one.

Weekly winner post template: Week [N], [Game]. Top score: [score], set by [handle] on [day]. The pot was $[amount], which is half the ad money this game made plus half of the merch. Paid [date]. Congrats, [handle]. Next pot starts at $[seed] and goes up from there, or does not.

Taglines
1. Half the ad money. All the shame.
2. Free games. Small pot. Real payout.
3. Someone has to win. Might as well be you, slightly.

## Honesty mechanics microcopy

Estimated (during week): "Estimated pot: $4.10. This is what the ad dashboard says right now. The network finalises numbers a few days after the week ends, so the real figure can move up or down." Dashed border or lighter type.
Confirmed (after close): "Confirmed pot: $3.40. Final. This is the number we are paying." Solid. Never show confirmed until the network reports.
Founder's seed, always a separate line: "Pot breakdown: $2.20 ad money + $0.00 merch + $5.00 founder's seed = $7.20." Footnote: "The seed is money the founder puts in so week one is not embarrassing. It is not ad revenue and we will say when it stops."
Rollover (unclaimed): "Nobody claimed week 12 on Slumlord. The winner had 72 hours to reply and did not. The $6.80 rolls into week 13, so next week's pot starts at $6.80 instead of $0. That is the only time a pot gets bigger without ads."
No ranked runs: "No ranked runs this week, so no winner. Pot rolls forward. Honestly a little hurtful."

## Who does this voice well

- Cards Against Humanity: the joke is always backed by a real verifiable number (the $71,145 Black Friday stunt, itemised). Borrow: publish the pot and payout like a receipt. https://techcrunch.com/2015/11/28/heres-what-cards-against-humanity-is-doing-with-the-71145-they-made-on-black-friday
- Oatly: voice written into the tiny constrained fields; pre-empts criticism by saying it first. https://chiefmarketer.com/how-oatly-uses-its-packaging-and-creative-to-stay-original/
- Liquid Death: commit fully to one dumb premise, never wink to apologise. https://www.printmag.com/?p=749543
- Wendy's 2017: punch at a claim, not a person; factual underneath. https://www.prdaily.com/wendys-dishes-snark-endears-consumers/
- Goat Simulator Steam page: tells you not to buy it, names the flaws first. Closest model for the game cards. https://store.steampowered.com/app/265930
- Patagonia "Don't Buy This Jacket": the "we don't need you to do this" posture, credible only if honesty costs you something. https://davidgelles.substack.com/p/the-secret-history-of-dont-buy-this
- Duolingo's unhinged era: rolled back by April 2026 after backlash. Deadpan ages better than mania. https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2026/04/23/duolingo-is-reportedly-rolling-back-its-unhinged-humor-marketing-strategy/

## Where this voice fails

The line is self-interest. Airbnb's 2015 SF signs read as entitled because Airbnb was the one benefiting. For 5050: the joke is always on 5050 or on web-ad economics, never on the person who won $3.40. https://www.marketsmiths.com/2016/badvertising-snarky-copywriting-gets-snotty/

Three more failure modes: punching down at a player; honesty that isn't (an "estimate" that never moves, seed blended into ad money); drift into unhinged.

Tone by page
- Homepage: full deadpan.
- Rules: one joke per section at most; this is the page people screenshot when angry.
- Payout email: warm and boringly precise, money details first, one dry line at the end.
- Cheating accusation: no jokes. Facts, evidence, 72-hour deadline, path to reply.
