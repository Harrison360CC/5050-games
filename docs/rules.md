# 5050 Games: Official Rules (draft v0.1, Oct 22, 2026)

Plain-language draft for the /rules page. Have a lawyer read it before the first payout.

## The deal

Three games. Each game has its own weekly leaderboard. When the week ends, the player with the highest verified score on each game wins 50% of the advertising revenue that game earned that week. The other 50% pays for the site and the person running it.

## What "ad revenue" means

The money Google actually pays us for ads shown inside that game during that week, after Google takes its cut. The number on the pot counter during the week is an estimate. It is replaced with the real figure when Google reports it, which is usually a few days after the week ends. The pot can go up or down at that point. We publish both numbers.

## Founder's pot

Until ad money is meaningful, we add a fixed amount to each game's pot out of our own pocket each week. The current amount is shown on the pot counter and labelled as such. We can change or stop this at any time, but never for a week that has already started.

## Weeks

A week runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59:59, UTC. Only runs that finish inside the week count for that week.

## Who can win

You must be 18 or older, have a 5050 account, and not be banned. Residents of Quebec can play but are not eligible for prizes. If you live in Canada you have to answer a skill-testing question before we pay you. Employees and family of 5050 Games can't win. Free to enter; nothing to buy, nothing to wager.

## How scores work

Only ranked runs count. Practice runs never touch the leaderboard. A ranked run is recorded as the list of taps you made and is replayed on our server to compute the score. The score your phone shows is not what we trust; the replay is. Your best verified run of the week is the one on the board. Ties go to whoever posted first.

## Cheating

If a replay doesn't reproduce, taps faster than a human, or anything else smells wrong, the run is void. We can void any run, ban any account and withhold any prize if we believe a run wasn't played by a human on a normal device, and we don't have to prove it to you. We keep every replay, including rejected ones. Past a pot of $500 the winner must also complete a verification run on video before being paid.

## Claiming

Winners get an email within 24 hours of the week closing and have 72 hours to claim by replying with a PayPal address or Interac e-transfer email. Unclaimed money rolls into next week's pot for the same game. Pots under $1 roll over automatically. We pay within 7 days of a valid claim.

## Ads

Interstitial ads show between runs, never during one. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch, and what you get is always in-game (an extra practice run, a cosmetic). Watching ads never changes a ranked score and is never a condition of winning. Ad views are capped per day. Please don't try to farm ads; it gets our ad account banned, which ends the prizes for everyone.

## Privacy

We store your account email, your handle, your replays, and the IP and device info of ranked runs. We don't sell any of it. Replays may be shown publicly as "watch the winning run." Full policy at /privacy.

## Changes

We can change these rules. Changes apply to future weeks, never the week in progress. Past versions are kept at /rules/history.
