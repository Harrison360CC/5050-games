Rules history

Every version of the rules, kept so nobody has to take my word for what they said last week.

Why this page exists

The rules say changes apply to future weeks and never the week in progress. That promise is worth nothing if the old wording disappears, so the old wording lives here. Nothing comes off this page. If a version is missing, that is a mistake and you should say so at play@play5050games.com.

v0.3 · Oct 23, 2026 · current

Four changes, all of them things the previous version got wrong rather than things I decided to change:

  • Prize value. v0.2 said the pot was "historically small". There is no history: the site opened on October 22, 2026 and no week has closed. Replaced with a plain statement that nothing has been paid out yet, plus the odds sentence that should have been there from the start.
  • Guaranteed minimum. v0.2 said the minimum was the seed "then in effect, if any", which voided the guarantee in the same sentence it offered it. Now the seed shown when a week opens is committed for that week and cannot be withdrawn from it.
  • Winner contact. v0.2 promised an email within 24 hours of the week closing. There is no automated email here and never has been; one person sends them by hand. A deadline I cannot guarantee is worse than a slower one I can. The 72 hour claim window now starts when I reach you rather than when the week ended, so a slow email cannot cost you the pot.
  • Sponsor eligibility. v0.2 said household members and employees of the sponsor were ineligible but never named the obvious case. I play my own games. My handle is 5050GameGod, it is now flagged ineligible in the database rather than only on this page, and it stays visible on the boards.
